Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Panbang Ecolodge ("we", "us", "our") is located at Anthala, Panbang, Zhemgang, Bhutan. This page explains what information we collect when you use our website and how we use it.

1. What Information We Collect

We only collect information you give us directly through our booking form or by contacting us. This includes:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • Your check-in and check-out dates
  • Number of guests and room type preference
  • Any special requests you choose to share

We do not use cookies, tracking scripts, or analytics tools on this website. We do not collect any information automatically.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to:

  • Respond to your booking inquiry
  • Confirm or discuss your reservation
  • Contact you about your stay at Panbang Ecolodge

We do not use your information for marketing, and we do not send newsletters or promotional emails.

3. Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. Your booking details stay between you and us.

Our booking form is processed through Netlify Forms. Netlify stores form submissions securely on our behalf. You can read Netlify's privacy policy at netlify.com/privacy.

4. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your booking inquiry for as long as it is needed to manage your reservation. If you did not complete a booking, we do not retain your information beyond our initial response.
