Experiences

Ways to lose track of time in Panbang!

Forest Melody

Each day breaks with wings stirring above Panbang. More than five hundred kinds of birds live here. Twenty two face extinction worldwide. This place, Zhemgang, slips beneath most eyes despite its weight in feathers and flight. Through foggy light, hornbills slice air while a Ward's Trogon answers from high trees. What unfolds every ordinary Tuesday draws watchers who cross oceans without hesitation.
